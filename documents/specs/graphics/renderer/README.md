The renderer games draw through, and the render graph it records each frame with. Its
milestones and later work are listed in the [ngen index](../../README.md).

## Specs

- [Renderer](renderer.md): the renderer configuration and stages, the per-frame render list, and the frame it renders from them.
- [Render graph](render_graph/render_graph.md): one frame's passes, ordered, with their barriers and load and store ops inferred from what they write and read.
